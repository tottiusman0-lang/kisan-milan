HELP

Help

How do I sell my crop?

Go to "Sell My Crop", add a photo, quantity and harvest date, then tap "List My Crop".

Do I need to send a physical sample?

Not always. Small or repeat orders usually only need your photo and quality history. First-time or large orders may require a sample.

When do I get paid?

Payment is sent to your registered bank account or UPI after delivery is confirmed.

I don't have a smartphone I'm confident using.

Ask your local FPO or facilitator for help — they can assist with listing, but you always see the offer and make the final decision.
